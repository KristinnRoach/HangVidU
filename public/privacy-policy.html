Privacy Policy for HangVidU

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains what HangVidU stores, why, and who can access it.

Account

You sign in with Google. We receive your name, email address, and profile picture, and use them to identify you and let other users find you.

Messages and files

Messages and files you send are stored on Cloudflare (D1 database and R2 file storage) so you can see your chat history. Cloudflare encrypts them at rest, but they are not end-to-end encrypted: the HangVidU operator can technically access them. We only do so when needed to run the service, fix a problem you report, or comply with the law. We do not use them for advertising or profiling. Deleted messages may remain in Cloudflare's database recovery history for up to 30 days.

Calls

Video and audio go directly between participants (peer-to-peer) and are never recorded or stored. A Cloudflare Worker briefly handles connection details, such as IP addresses, to set up the call; these are not stored.

Optional Google Contacts and Gmail access

Only if you choose to import contacts, we request read-only access to your Google Contacts and "Other contacts" to show them in HangVidU and see who already uses it. Only if you choose to send an email invite, we request Gmail send access to send that invite. We never read your email. Google access tokens stay in your browser's memory and are cleared when you sign out. Google contact data is only used during the import and is not stored. All data is sent over HTTPS. We do not sell or share Google data or use it for advertising.

HangVidU's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking Access and Data Deletion

You can revoke HangVidU's access to your Google data at any time by visiting your Google Account permissions page.

To request deletion of your HangVidU account data (including saved contacts and chat history stored on our servers), please contact us at kristinnroach@gmail.com.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase: For hosting, authentication, online presence, and push notifications.
  • Cloudflare: For message and file storage, contacts, and call signaling.
  • YouTube API: If you use the shared video watching feature, your use is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: kristinnroach@gmail.com
